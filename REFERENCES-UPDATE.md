These references support the stated claims, not every numerical or clinical detail in each case. GDC is the professional council; NICE/SDCEP are clinical guidance bodies, RCS supplies exam specifications and clinical guidance, and other primary organisations are named explicitly. The historical verification report remains separate. No source-extractor line numbers are used as published locators. Printed pages and PDF-viewer pages are distinguished where provided.
